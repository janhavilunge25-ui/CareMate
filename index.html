About CareMate

Healthy habits, shaped around you

A Personalized Diet, Exercise and Wellness Recommendation System.

Why we built it

General health advice rarely fits everyone. A person with PCOD, high blood pressure or diabetes needs different food and movement from someone who just wants to stay fit.

CareMate brings diet, yoga and meditation together in one simple place. You share a few details, and it prepares an easy daily routine that you can follow, track and adjust.

The food guidance in CareMate is prepared with our team dietitian, Dhanashree Pundkar, so every suggestion is practical, simple and easy to follow.

Personal

Simple

Short plans and videos that are easy to start and keep.

Safe

General wellness guidance that never replaces medical advice.

Is CareMate a replacement for my doctor?

No. CareMate gives general wellness guidance only. Please check with your doctor or dietitian.

How is my plan created?

Your plan is based on your profile, such as food preference, activity level and condition.

Will I get the same food every day?

No. Your diet plan has a different menu for each of 7 days, so you can enjoy variety and keep going. After Day 7 it starts again.

Which health conditions are covered?

Hypertension and hypotension (high and low blood pressure), diabetes and blood sugar, thyroid, PCOD and PCOS, anaemia, low immunity, arthritis and bone health, pregnancy and lactation, child health, detox therapy, kidney stone, cancer malnutrition, IBD, obesity management and weight gain, plus general wellness.

Can I ask the dietitian a question?

Yes. Open your plan and choose “Ask a Question” in Your areas, or send a message from this Contact section.

Can I choose what I see?

Yes. Tick only the areas you want help with, and your plan shows just those.
